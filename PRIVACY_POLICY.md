# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 30, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Athelix ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

## 2. Information We Collect

**Account Information:** When you register, we collect your email address and username.

**Fitness Data:** We collect workout data you enter, including exercises, sets, reps, weight measurements, bodyweight entries, and progression history.

**Device Information:** We collect device type, operating system version, and app version for analytics and troubleshooting.

**Usage Data:** We use PostHog to collect anonymous usage data such as screen views, feature interactions, and app performance metrics. This helps us improve the app.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- To provide and maintain your account and workout data
- To sync your data across devices
- To improve the app based on usage patterns
- To diagnose technical issues

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on our servers and transmitted using encryption. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

## 5. Third-Party Services

We use PostHog for product analytics. PostHog processes anonymous usage data and does not receive your email, username, or workout data.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your account data until you delete your account. Analytics data is retained for 12 months. You can request deletion of your data at any time.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your data
- Export your data
- Withdraw consent for analytics

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you of changes via the app or email.
