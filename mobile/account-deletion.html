Delete Your Account

Option 1 — In-App (recommended)

Open Athelix → Settings → scroll to "Danger Zone" → tap "Delete Account" → confirm.

Your data is deleted immediately. This cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Email Request

Send an email to support@athelix.fit from the email address registered on your account with the subject line "Delete My Account".

We will process your request within 7 days.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your profile and account credentials
  • All workout history, sets, reps, and weights
  • Body weight logs and progress data
  • Personal records and achievement data

Data Retention

Backup copies may persist for up to 30 days before permanent erasure. Analytics data (PostHog) is anonymized and cannot be linked back to you after deletion.
